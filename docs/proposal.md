## About us

[SAMIZDATA](https://samizdata.co/) is a data storytelling consultancy, led by [Nicu Calcea](https://nicu.md/), a journalist with 16 years' experience in media.

Nicu has worked as a data journalist for the [New Statesman](https://nicu.md/work?publication=New+Statesman), [BBC News](https://nicu.md/work?publication=BBC+News), [Global Witness](https://nicu.md/work?publication=Global+Witness) and others, with additional work done for VICE, Transport & Environment, and infogr8. The team he was leading won the "Most Creative Use of Data Journalism" award at [newsawards 2021](https://www.inpublishing.co.uk/articles/newsawards-2021-winners-announced-19107#:~:text=Most%20Creative%20Use%20of%20Data%20Journalism).

Before becoming a data journalist, he worked as a journalist for media organisations in Moldova, including Publika TV, Privesc.Eu, Moldova.org, and RISE Moldova.

Nicu also taught an "Introduction to Data Journalism" course at City, University of London between 2021 to 2025.
